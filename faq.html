Everything you need to know about temporary email.

Temporary email (also called temp mail, disposable email, or fake email) is a short-lived email address that you can use instead of your real address. It receives messages for a limited time and then expires, helping protect your primary inbox from spam and unwanted contact.

By default, a TempMal address remains active for a limited period. You can extend the lifetime using the controls on the homepage — options range from 10 minutes to 2 weeks. After expiration, the address and all associated messages are automatically deleted.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TempMal ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our temporary email service at tempmal.example.com (the "Service").

Information We Collect

TempMal is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require registration or personal information to use the Service. We may collect:

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and access timestamps stored in server logs for security and abuse prevention.
  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics about page visits and feature usage, if enabled.
  • Contact form data: Name, email, and message content if you voluntarily submit our contact form.

Temporary Email Data

When you use TempMal, we create a temporary email address and store incoming messages for the duration of the mailbox's lifetime. This data includes sender addresses, subject lines, message content, and any attachments.

All temporary email data is automatically deleted when the mailbox expires or is manually deleted by the user. We do not retain expired mailbox content.

Cookies

We may use essential cookies and local storage to maintain your session, store your current mailbox identifier, and remember preferences. We do not use cookies for targeted advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some features may not function correctly.

Server Logs

Like most web services, our servers automatically record certain information including IP addresses, request URLs, HTTP status codes, and timestamps. Logs are retained for a limited period for security monitoring and troubleshooting, then deleted.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party infrastructure providers (hosting, CDN) to deliver the Service. These providers process data on our behalf under contractual obligations. Our website may contain links to external sites; we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Data Retention

Temporary email messages and mailbox data are retained only for the active lifetime of the mailbox. Server logs are retained for up to 90 days. Contact form submissions may be retained until the inquiry is resolved.

Data Deletion

You can delete your mailbox and all associated messages at any time using the Delete button on the homepage. Expired mailboxes are automatically purged from our systems. For more information about how TempMal works, see our about page or FAQ.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Temporary email should not be used for highly sensitive communications.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us through our contact form.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our contact form.
